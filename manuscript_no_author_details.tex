\title{Beyond Formality: The Firm-Size Wage Premium in Colombia}
\author{}
\date{}

\begin{document}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This paper documents the firm-size wage premium in Colombia using household survey data for 2008--2025. The full sample includes salaried workers, own-account workers, employers, and other workers with positive labor income. We regress log real hourly labor income on firm-size indicators interacted with formality, controlling for worker characteristics, occupational position, and sector-department-year fixed effects. We repeat the analysis for a salaried workers sample. Relative to solo workers, the premium in firms with over 100 workers is 37 percent for informal and 17 percent for formal workers in the full sample. In the salaried workers sample, it is about 20 percent for both formal and informal workers. Reallocating all workers in each sample to formal employment in firms with over 100 workers would raise predicted average labor income by at most 25.4 percent in the full sample and 11.9 percent in the salaried workers sample. Despite substantial predicted gains for reassigned workers, nearly all of Colombia's productivity gap with the United States would remain.
\end{abstract}
