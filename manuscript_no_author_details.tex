\title{Beyond Formality: The Firm-Size Wage Premium in Colombia}
\author{}
\date{}

\begin{document}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This paper documents the firm-size wage premium in Colombia using household survey data for 2008--2025. We regress log real hourly labor income on firm-size indicators interacted with formality status, controlling for sex, age, age squared, education, occupational position, and sector-department-year fixed effects. Relative to solo workers of the same formality status, workers in firms with more than 100 workers earn about 37 percent more within informal employment and 17 percent more within formal employment, conditional on these controls. The firm-size wage premium is therefore more pronounced among informal workers. Illustrative worker reallocation scenarios imply substantial average income gains for reassigned workers. The increase in total labor income is nevertheless modest relative to Colombia's productivity gap with the United States. The premium is consistent with labor-market frictions that may constrain the growth of small businesses and limit worker reallocation toward larger, potentially more productive firms.
\end{abstract}
