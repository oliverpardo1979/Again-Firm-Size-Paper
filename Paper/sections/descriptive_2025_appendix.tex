\section{Additional Tables}
\label{app:additional-tables}

This appendix reports the minimum-wage distribution tables, alternative-reference tables, and comparison specifications referenced in the main text. Table~\ref{tab:minimum-wage-bunching-2025} complements Figure~\ref{fig:minwage-distribution-formality-size} by quantifying the share of workers below, near, and above the statutory hourly minimum wage in 2025. Table~\ref{tab:minimum-wage-monthly-bunching-2025} reports the analogous exercise using implied monthly labor income relative to the monthly minimum wage. The alternative-reference tables use workers in firms with 101 or more workers or with 11--19 workers as the reference category. These changes alter the normalization but do not change the underlying empirical specifications. In each table, the sign of a coefficient indicates whether the listed category is above or below the omitted category under the same controls.

Table~\ref{tab:firm-size-local-geography-robustness} adds geographic and occupational-position controls to the common-premium comparison model. Its final column absorbs sector-department-year fixed effects and controls for occupational position. It constrains the size premium to be the same for formal and informal workers. Under this restriction, workers in firms with 101 or more employees earn about 40.2 percent more than comparable solo workers, a statistically significant difference. Tables~\ref{tab:firm-size-local-geography-robustness-101-reference} and \ref{tab:firm-size-local-geography-robustness-11-19-reference} report the same models with alternative reference categories. The preferred model relaxes the common-premium restriction and is reported in Table~\ref{tab:firm-size-local-geography-formality-robustness} in the main text.
